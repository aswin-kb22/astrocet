Who we are

Vision & mission

From a humble hobby club in 2019 to a beacon of celestial exploration.

🔭
Vision

AstroCET aims to lead in astronomy education and outreach, inspiring students and the public to explore the cosmos. By fostering skill development, collaborating with institutions, and promoting innovation, we seek to create an inclusive environment and contribute to scientific knowledge.

🚀
Mission

Our primary mission is to ignite a passion for astronomy among students and the general public, providing a platform for learning, exploration, and innovation. From solar observation to AI-powered space research, AstroCET bridges curiosity and science.
